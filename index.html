SPATIAL INTERACTION

Three Ways
to Control

Switch freely between modes designed for different users and contexts

01 Touch 02 Natural 03 Power
01

Touch Mode

Look at it, reach out, and touch it. An invisible control volume around the index finger enables natural interaction with floating UI panels in 3D space.

  • Tactile response for a physical feel
  • Gaze dwell for relaxed device selection
  • Floating panels anchored to real devices
For new & casual users
02

Natural Mode

Quick control at a glance. Device icons floating in 3D space with color pickers and sliders using standard visionOS look-and-pinch interaction.

  • Standard visionOS interaction pattern
  • Device-locked windows reduce clutter
  • Rich visual information about device state
Default mode for most users
03

Power Mode

Custom multi-hand gestures for rapid control with minimal visual clutter. Wrist tap, conductor wave, prayer hands — and more gestures coming.

  • Wrist Tap — open settings panel
  • Conductor — gesture-driven control
  • Prayer Hands — scene selector
For power users & enthusiasts
